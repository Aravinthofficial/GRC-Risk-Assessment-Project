# GRC Risk Assessment & Compliance Tracker (Fresher Portfolio Project)

## Project objective
Demonstrate a beginner-level Governance, Risk & Compliance workflow using a fictional organization and Microsoft Excel.

## Folder contents
- `GRC_Risk_Assessment_Tracker.xlsx` — Risk Register, Dashboard, and Read Me worksheet.
- `docs/Project_Guide_Tamil_English.md` — Step-by-step project explanation, methodology, and interview preparation.
- `templates/Risk_Assessment_Template.csv` — Blank starter template for adding your own risks.

## How to use
1. Open the Excel workbook in Microsoft Excel.
2. Read the `Read Me` worksheet first.
3. Review the fictional examples in `Risk Register`.
4. Add or edit risks based on your own lab scenario.
5. Update controls, treatment, owner, due date, status, and residual scores.
6. Check the `Dashboard` for risk counts.
7. Prepare to explain your assumptions and limitations in an interview.

## Important
This is a simulated learning project. It does not represent a real company's audit, formal risk acceptance, or ISO 27001 certification. In real environments, scoring thresholds and risk acceptance criteria must be approved by the organization.
